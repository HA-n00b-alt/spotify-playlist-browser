## Context

What forced a decision: the problem, the constraints, and the options that were on the table.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated so a reader can tell whether a later change follows it or breaks it.

## Consequences

What this makes easier and harder, what it costs, and what follow-up work it creates (link issues).
